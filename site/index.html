Freedom Fest runs on favors

Free entry, free parking, free bands, free camping. Four days of that runs on people noticing what's missing and bringing it. Pitch In gives every need one live board that anyone can see, so whoever has the ice, the extra hands, or the spare cords can grab the job.

  1. 1

    Spot it

    A crew posts what it needs: 20 bags of ice for the kitchen by 3:00.

  2. 2

    Claim it

    You tap Claim and say how many. The bar fills, so nobody else buys the same bags.

  3. 3

    Bring it

    Drop it where the map pin points. The kitchen marks it done.

Bring five bags. Someone else has the other fifteen.

Every crew posts what it needs before, during, and after Freedom Fest: supplies, hands, skills, and rides. Claim the whole thing or just a slice, and the progress bar shows what's still open. Try the claim button on the phone.

  • Critical needs sit at the top, then whatever's due soonest.
  • Quick filters: things you can bring from home, tasks under 30 minutes, and needs due in the next 2 hours.
  • Labor needs show open slots, like 2 of 4 people for dish crew.
  • Anyone can browse. Claiming takes a display name, and nicknames are welcome.

A schedule that keeps up with the stage.

Both stages, all four days, with what's on now and what's next right on the home screen. When a set runs long or moves, the stage manager taps "+15 min" and everyone who starred that set gets a heads-up.

  • Timetable grid, day lists, and a page for every performer.
  • Star the sets you want. Get a nudge 15 minutes before, and a warning if two overlap.
  • Import the whole lineup from a spreadsheet in one step.
  • Sunset times included, so you know when to grab a headlamp.

Every camera in the crowd feeds one album.

Anyone signed in can add photos to the Freedom Fest album and download everyone else's. Each shot lands on the right day and set on its own, credited to the person who took it, so everybody goes home with the whole weekend instead of just their own camera roll.

  • Upload a stack from your camera roll. Uploads keep going if the signal drops.
  • Photo times match the schedule, so a shot from 5:20 on Saturday suggests Howard Levy Duo on the Main Stage.
  • Hearts build a Crowd favorites set, and every photo downloads at full resolution.
  • Location data is stripped, and anyone can have a photo they're in taken down.

A channel for every crew.

Kitchen, lights and sound, bands, security, parking: each crew gets its own channel, so the kitchen never hears about sound check. Share a need into the chat and it stays live, updating as people claim it.

  • A leads channel for crew leads and coordinators, plus direct messages.
  • Pinned info at the top: radio channels, key contacts, and the current plan.
  • Photos, threads, and a 👍 that means "got it."
  • Crew leads can moderate their own channel.

Find the ice before the sun does.

The Freedom Fest site map becomes the base layer, with stages, drop-off points, water, restrooms, first aid, and a pin for every open need. Pins are colored by urgency. Tap one to see the need and claim it on the spot.

  • Crews post a need by tapping its spot on the map.
  • Pins vanish when a need is done, so the map only shows what's still open.
  • Later: a "you are here" dot and distance to each pin.

Built to start small

The first season covers the essentials for attendees and crews. The extras can wait until the crews ask for them.

First season

  • Live needs board with partial claims, statuses, and filters
  • Crew, leads, and direct-message channels with live need cards and pinned info
  • Site map with named spots and tap-to-claim need pins
  • Schedule with now and next, a timetable grid, and live delay updates
  • Event-wide alerts with a confirmation step
  • Lost and found listings with in-app messaging
  • Merch storefront with booth pickup codes
  • Shared Freedom Fest album with set tagging, credits, full-resolution downloads, and remove-me requests
  • Info pages, a searchable FAQ, and announcements
  • Crew invite links and codes, with open or approved joining

After that

  • Check-in pings that reopen forgotten claims
  • Escalation for critical needs nobody has claimed
  • Need templates and duplicate warnings for crews
  • A "you are here" dot on the site map
  • Starred sets with reminders and overlap warnings
  • Voice messages, search, and cross-crew channels
  • Lost-and-found matching and verification questions
  • Merch pre-orders, a booth card reader, and a sales dashboard
  • Organizer picks, self-tagging, and zipped album downloads
  • Rejoin invites that carry crews into next year

Display names only

The public sees display names only. Phone numbers and emails stay with the leads of the crew you're helping.

One home for the chatter

Messaging is built in, not borrowed from another app, so every need and its conversation stay together.

Free stays free

There are no tickets in the app. Crew shirts cost nothing, and only optional merch has a price.

Be kind to each other.

Free entry, free parking, free bands, free camping. Four days every Labor Day weekend, Friday through Monday.
